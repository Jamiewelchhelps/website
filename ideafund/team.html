Team

A small team that has been doing this together for a long time.

Our partners have been investing in the region since 2003, first at the MCNC Enterprise Fund and, since 2007, at IDEA Fund Partners. Most of the team lives within a short drive of the founders we back.

Venture Fellows

The rest of the table.

Each year a cohort of Venture Fellows works alongside the investment team on sourcing, diligence and portfolio support. It is a working apprenticeship, not a coffee-fetching program, and it is how we help build the region’s next generation of investors.
