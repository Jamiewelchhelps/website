01 The firm

Great companies get started in Durham, Charlotte, Atlanta and Richmond for the same reasons they get started anywhere: deep talent and a real problem worth solving. What is scarce in this part of the country is not ambition. It is early capital.

We have spent nearly two decades closing that gap, one first check at a time.

02 How we invest
01

First money in

We lead and co-lead seed rounds, and for most of our companies we are the first institutional name on the cap table. That means we underwrite people and product, not momentum.

02

Close to the ground

Our team has worked in the region’s startup communities for decades, which is how the best opportunities find us before they find a pitch list. We are down the road, not on the next flight out.

03

Built for the long haul

Company building at this stage is measured in years, not quarters. We reserve capital for follow-on rounds and take board work seriously, especially in the years when nothing goes to plan.
